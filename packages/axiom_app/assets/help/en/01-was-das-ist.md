# What this is

AXIOM measures your state, applies rules you set yourself, and names **exactly one** next action — together with the rule that produced it.

That is the whole mechanism. No recommender, no model, no AI: if-then sentences in plain language that you can read, change and switch off.

## How it differs from a to-do app

| Usual app | AXIOM |
|---|---|
| asks what you want to do | measures what state you are in |
| sorts by importance | sorts by start energy against available capacity |
| shows the whole list | shows one action; the list stays reachable |
| explains nothing | names the rule and its reasoning on every output |
| rewards usage | caps its own usage at twelve minutes a day |

## The four laws it is built on

**G1 — Offload, never demand.** No screen that forces you to think. Capture under three seconds, check-in under fifteen, daily review under two minutes.

**G2 — Explainable, not clever.** Every output carries a rule number. Every number unfolds and shows how it was calculated.

**G3 — Channel, do not suppress.** The need for stimulation gets scheduled, not judged. No bans — only waiting time, visibility and alternatives.

**G4 — Self-limitation.** AXIOM caps its own screen time and locks its own configuration once the budget is used up.

## What it is not

- not a habit tracker, no streaks, no points
- not a coach — it does not motivate, it executes
- no account, no cloud, nobody else looking at your numbers
- not a medical device, not diagnostics, not therapy

The measure of success is accordingly not "more done" but: the same output at a lower cost. What follows from that, and what it rules out, is in [Limits](kapitel:13).
