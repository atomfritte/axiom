# The first day

Six onboarding pages, one first check-in, then fourteen days of measuring. What runs during that time — and what does not yet.

## Onboarding

Six pages, every one skippable:

1. What AXIOM is — and what it does not do
2. State, rule, one action
3. The capacity line
4. The first check-in
5. Three system permissions
6. Sleep and steps from Health Connect

Page 4 is the only one where something happens: four sliders, roughly is enough. Without a first reading AXIOM has nothing to calculate with and would be guessing.

The three permissions on page 5 are not a formality. AXIOM works with exact times; without the exact-alarm permission a prompt arrives forty minutes late or not at all. Health Connect on page 6 is optional — without it AXIOM keeps calculating from your check-ins, just less precisely.

All of it can be changed later. *System → System check* shows what the device has actually granted, and takes you to the right place for whatever is missing.

## The baseline

The baseline starts when onboarding finishes. It has three conditions, and all three have to be met:

| Condition | Target |
|---|---|
| Days | 14 |
| Readings (check-ins) | 20 |
| Nights (sleep entries) | 7 |

![System → Calibration shows three separate conditions instead of one percentage bar.](img/eichung.webp)

Three numbers rather than one percentage, because "73 % baseline" does not tell you whether readings or nights are missing — and that is exactly what decides where to put the effort.

## What runs during those fourteen days

Rules that look at clock times and counted events work from day one: the three check-in prompts, the body reminders late morning and afternoon, the evening cutoff, the end of day. Those quantities are exact, not estimated.

Rules that look at calculated values — capacity, compensation load, stimulation need — run on weights that are plausible guesses. They are marked **UNCALIBRATED** in the rulebook, with wider intervals and lower daily limits. A recommendation built on a guess costs trust that you do not get back.

Once all three conditions are met, the main view says so by itself. What happens then is in [Review and calibration](kapitel:10).
