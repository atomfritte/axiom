# Limits

What AXIOM cannot do because the device does not allow it — and what it does not want to be because it would defeat the purpose.

## What the device does not allow

**Air Actions do not exist on the Galaxy S25 Ultra.** Its S Pen has no Bluetooth, so remote control, pairing and charging are all gone — for every app, not just AXIOM. What remains: pull out the pen, tap AXIOM in the Air Command menu. Registered as a shortcut, that is two moves.

**Screen-off memos land in Samsung Notes.** There is no public interface for them. Any route there would be reverse engineering, and it would not survive the next system update.

**"Hey Google, note in AXIOM" does not work.** Assistant voice commands require the app to be distributed through Google Play; with a self-installed app Google does not verify the signature. What does work: the microphone in the capture field, a Bixby routine, or a link to `axiom://capture?text=…`

**Lock screen widgets do not exist on Android** — removed with Android 5.0. The persistent notification is the remaining route to permanent visibility while locked.

**The Now Bar is not guaranteed.** There is no Samsung interface for it; One UI fills it from the Live Updates of Android 16. For time-bounded operations the request is accepted reliably, for a permanent display not always. How it turned out on your device is shown under *System → System check*.

**Health Connect only exists on Android.** On the desktop AXIOM calculates from your check-ins — same rules, one source fewer.

## What AXIOM does not want to be

- **No AI in the decision loop.** A suggestion you cannot recalculate cannot be audited — and a system that cannot be audited is rightly discarded.
- **No streaks, no points, no comparison with others.** A broken streak produces abandonment rather than correction.
- **No server sync.** Two devices align over an encrypted file, see [Your data](kapitel:12).
- **No endless configurability.** Every extra setting is fuel for working on the system instead of in your life.
- **Not a medical device.** No diagnostics, no therapy, no clinical thresholds. Compensation load measures a state and diagnoses nothing.

## And what is still simply guessed

Until calibration, capacity, compensation load and stimulation need rest on plausibly estimated weights. Affected rules carry the **UNCALIBRATED** tag. They can be wrong — that tag is there so you know it, not so you believe it.

The way out is in [Review and calibration](kapitel:10).
