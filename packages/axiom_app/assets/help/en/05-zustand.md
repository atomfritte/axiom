# State

Six readings, each one unfolding into how it was calculated. They describe a state — they do not grade you and they diagnose nothing.

![State: six values, each with a one-sentence reading.](img/zustand.webp)

## The six readings

| Reading | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Capacity | how much executive reserve is there today |
| Compensation load | accumulated effort of holding the day together |
| Stimulation need | an uncovered need finds the fastest channel |
| Focus load today | concentration time spent since this morning |
| Regulation reserve | buffer for emotional strain |
| Sleep debt | the single strongest modulator of capacity |

Capacity decides the most: it determines which tasks are shown at all. It is made up of sleep debt (30 %), compensation load (25 %), focus load (20 %), regulation reserve (15 %) and the shape of your day (10 %).

## Where the numbers come from

Three sources, and no others: the check-ins (three a day, four sliders, two more in the evening), the sleep entries, and — if granted — sleep windows and steps from Health Connect. On top of that, events AXIOM keeps anyway: focus minutes, covered stimulation slots, acknowledged body prompts.

Each reading carries a confidence. When the data is too thin, rules stay quiet rather than guess.

## The four load levels

Compensation load has levels with real consequences inside the system:

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| L0 | nothing, normal operation |
| L1 | focus blocks capped at 75 minutes |
| L2 | capped at 50 minutes, new commitments need a confirmation, suggestions only up to start energy 6 |
| L3 | 72 hours of obligations and recovery only, optional things hidden, blocks capped at 30 minutes |

L3 is deliberately uncomfortable. A highly compensated system is the last to notice its own collapse, because from the outside it runs without error until shortly before the break. That this level engages is the purpose of the system — not your failure.

If L3 holds for more than fourteen days, a note appears: AXIOM only measures; for an actual assessment, medical or psychotherapeutic advice is the right route.

## What these numbers are not

Not a diagnosis, not a finding, not a screening score. And until calibration the weights come from plausible estimates rather than from your data — see [Review and calibration](kapitel:10).
